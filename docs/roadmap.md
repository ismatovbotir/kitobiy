# Kitobiy roadmap

*For the marketing team. Last updated: 3 November 2026.*

Kitobiy is a catalog for home libraries. It started with a simple problem: a family kept buying books they already owned. The aim is to grow it, step by step, into a network around books: families, friends, collectors, publishers and bookshops.

**Status at a glance**

| Phase | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Our home library | ✅ Built, in family testing |
| 1.2 | A shared book catalog, kept clean | ✅ Built |
| 1.5 | Ready for public launch | 🟡 In progress (6 of ~8 parts done) |
| 2 | Labels, QR codes and lending | 🔜 Next. Label designs already exist |
| 3 | Community | Planned |
| 4 | Business | Planned |

> **Please don't announce dates.** Kitobiy is not open to the public yet. The phases are listed in order; how long each one takes depends on real-use testing.

---

## Phase 1: Our home library ✅

*"Stop buying the same book twice."*

- **Add a book in seconds.** Scan the barcode with your phone camera or type the ISBN, and Kitobiy fills in the title, authors, publisher and cover. Books without a barcode can be added by hand.
- **One library for the whole family.** Invite family members with a link you can send over Telegram. No email is needed.
- **Find any book.** Search by title, author, translator or ISBN. Filter by genre, tag or language.
- **Spot duplicates.** Kitobiy shows the books you own twice: the same edition, or the same book in another edition or translation.
- **Your own tags.** For example "Gift from grandmother", "To read" or "Signed by the author".
- **Three languages.** Uzbek (Latin) first, then Russian and English.
- **Warm, simple design**, made for readers rather than for librarians.

## Phase 1.2: A shared book catalog ✅

*"Every book added helps the next reader."*

- When one family adds a book, the next family with the same book finds it ready to use. Over time this builds a **crowd-sourced catalog of Uzbek books**, which many online databases are missing.
- **Well-known writers and publishers are already in the catalog,** imported from Wikidata in Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic), Russian and English. The first run brought in 132 Uzbek writers and 13 publishers. Neighbouring countries come next.
- **Translators, compilers, editors and illustrators** are credited, not only authors.
- A small admin team keeps the catalog tidy: it fixes spellings, merges duplicate entries and manages genres.

## Phase 1.5: Ready for public launch 🟡

**Done:**
- **Searches that understand Uzbek.** `Cho'lpon`, `Choʻlpon` and `Чўлпон` find the same writer. Apostrophes, capital letters and Latin or Cyrillic letters no longer matter.
- **More books found automatically.** If one book database doesn't know a book, Kitobiy checks a second one (Google Books).
- **Several libraries per person.** For example home, the summer house or grandparents' shelves, with a quick switch between them.
- **Roles in a library.** *Owner*, *Administrator*, *Member* (adds books) and *Viewer* (only looks: good for grandparents or young children).
- **Email confirmation** for new accounts, which keeps the service safe before it opens to everyone.
- **Gift page.** Turn on a private link and send it to friends. They see your books (titles, authors, covers, never your notes or tags) and can type a title to check: "They already have this" or "It could make a good gift!" No account needed.

**Still to come:**
- **Your own cover photos** for books that have no cover online.
- **The real Kitobiy logo.** It must be simple enough to print small on a book label.

## Phase 2: Labels, QR codes and lending 🔜

*"Every book knows its way home."*

- **Ex libris labels.** Print stickers for your books with the library name, title and a QR code. Three label sizes are already designed for standard A4 sticker sheets.
- **Lost and found.** Anyone who finds a book can scan its QR code and send a message to the owner. The owner's contact details stay private.
- **Lending.** Keep track of who borrowed a book and when it's due back, including friends who don't use Kitobiy.
- **Giving books away.** Pass a book to another library for good, and it keeps its history ("from the X family library").
- **A Telegram bot** for invitations and notifications.

## Phase 3: Community

- A place for **collectors** to share their collections.
- **Sell or swap** second-hand books.
- **Messages** between readers.

## Phase 4: Business

- **Libraries for offices and schools.**
- A **label printing service**: we design, print and deliver labels, paid with local payment methods.
- **News and offers from publishers and bookshops.**
- **"Buy as a gift"** through partner bookshops, starting from a friend's gift page.

---

## Messages that work well

- **The problem everyone knows:** "Did we already buy this book?"
- **Made for Uzbek readers:** Uzbek first, Latin and Cyrillic both understood, and Uzbek writers already in the catalog.
- **A family project:** the whole family builds one library together, and even grandparents can join as viewers.
- **Readers help readers:** every book you add makes the catalog better for everyone.

## Please don't promise yet

- Launch dates, or that Kitobiy is open to the public.
- The iPhone barcode scanner (not yet tested on a real iPhone).
- Prices, paid label printing, or marketplace features (Phases 3–4).
